# Code of conduct

🇬🇧 **English** · 🇩🇪 [Deutsch](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.de.md)

Nestwerk is a project for families. We want everyone who takes part to feel welcome, whatever their background, experience or language. This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).

## Our standard

Good behavior:

- Be kind, patient and respectful.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Assume good intent and ask when something is unclear.
- Write in English or German; help each other across languages.
- Keep discussions on the topic of the project.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or content, and any content unsuitable for a family project.
- Publishing other people's private information, especially data about children, without consent.
- Spam, trolling and sustained disruption.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and to public situations where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that break this code, and may restrict participants who do. To report a problem, use the [private vulnerability reporting channel](https://github.com/hassio0815-tech/nestwerk/security) and mark it as a conduct report, or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately and in good faith.
