# Nestwerk: User Guide

🇬🇧 **English** · 🇩🇪 [Deutsch](../BENUTZERHANDBUCH.md)

For **parents** and **kids**. Short, with the most important steps.

The values in this guide are **default values**. Parents can adjust all points, prices and thresholds in the settings.

---

## 1. Basic idea

- **Voluntary:** every task is optional. There is no obligation, no punishment and no point deductions.
- **Taking part pays off:** whoever completes tasks earns points. You can spend points on rewards, mods or special days.
- **You decide:** you choose which tasks you take on and what you spend your points on.
- **Everything stays in the family:** the app runs on your own server in the home network.

---

## 2. Tasks and approval

**For kids**

1. On the kids' dashboard, select a task under **Open tasks**.
2. You accept it: it is reserved for you. Your siblings see it as taken.
3. Done? **Report as done.** If needed, add a photo or a message.
4. Your parents approve it. Then you get the points.

**Several interested kids:** if several kids want the same task, the parents decide who gets it.

**Reservation:** whoever accepts a task holds it reserved. Once the time has run out, it becomes free again. There is no penalty for this.

**For parents**

- Create tasks: title and point value are required. Everything else is optional (time window, repeat, assignment, limit).
- Rate a task: one point is roughly one minute of effort. This is a guideline.
- Approval: **Trust** (points right away), **Parents approve** or **Photo/text proof**.
- Bonus points: after approval, you can give extra points with a short reason (e.g. "very thorough").
- Proofs (photos and texts) are deleted immediately after approval.
- The **task library** contains templates. One click activates them.

---

## 3. Points, balance and XP

- **Points:** every approved task brings points.
- **Balance:** the points you can spend right now. They go down when you redeem something or buy a mod.
- **XP (total points):** they rise with every task and **never fall**. Your rank is based on your XP.

**For kids:** your balance is your wallet. Your XP is your progress.

---

## 4. Rewards and pocket money

- **Reward catalog:** parents set fixed rewards with prices in points. Categories: Sweets, TV time, Gaming and computer time, Pocket money, Other.
- **Redeeming:** you submit a **request**. Both parents are notified right away.
- **Points are deducted only on approval:** the points are taken off only once a parent agrees.
- **Pending requests** already count against your balance. You cannot request more than you have.
- **Pocket money:** there is a **monthly limit** per child. Parents set it.
- Parents unlock devices, gaming or TV time outside the app.

---

## 5. Ranks

There are six ranks, like rarities in a game:

| Rank | From XP (start) | Minimum active weeks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Common | 0 | 0 |
| 2 Uncommon | 2,000 | 20 |
| 3 Rare | 5,000 | 42 |
| 4 Epic | 9,000 | 64 |
| 5 Legendary | 14,000 | 90 |
| 6 God Mode | 20,000 | 120 |

- **Rank up:** you need enough XP **and** enough **active weeks**.
- **Active week:** a week in which at least one of your tasks was approved.
- Active weeks are never reset. Weeks without a task cost nothing, but they don't count either.
- Age doesn't matter. The same rules apply to everyone.
- **God Mode:** after that, every 26 active weeks you get a special day and a new mod set, endlessly.

---

## 6. Streak and break

- **Weekly streak:** every active week in a row counts +1.
- **Streak bonus:** from streak 2 on, you get more points per task. +5% per streak week, up to a maximum of +25%.
- **If a week is missed,** the streak starts again at 0. Points, rank and active weeks stay.
- **Daily streak:** days in a row with an approved task. It earns badges, but no points.
- **Break:** parents set a break for one child or the whole family. Break weeks and days are neutral. The streak does not break. You see "Break – your streak is safe".
- Tasks completed during a break count as normal.

---

## 7. Badges

- **Badges** are awards for streaks, quarters and rank-ups.
- Examples: daily streak of 7, 14 or 30 days. Weekly streak of 1, 2, 4, 8, 12, 24 or 48 weeks. A quarter with active weeks throughout. A badge for every rank-up.
- **Badges give no points.** They appear on your profile. A pop-up announces new badges.

---

## 8. Avatar, mods and building

- **Avatar:** every child has their own block figure. You choose a **theme**: Knight, Space explorer, Ninja, Robot, Pirate, Viking, Footballer, Basketball player, Racing driver, or Jungle or forest explorer.
- **Six slots:** head, body, back, hand, companion and background.
- **Mods** are purely cosmetic. They have no effect on points or rules. You pay for them with your balance.
- **Mod shop:** you buy mods for your rank. A mod can only be bought once you reach the rank whose rarity it has. Prices rise with rarity.
- **Build time:** a bought mod takes time (e.g. 2 days for Common, 7 days for Legendary). Only then does it count toward your **build level**.
- **Build level:** the number of finished mods in your current rank (max. 6). At most one finished mod counts per week.
- **Changing theme:** possible at any time. You keep your rank, but the mods in your slots are lost. A warning shows you the exact numbers beforehand. There is no refund.

---

## 9. Hall of Fame

- **Unlocking:** you reach build level 6 in your current rank (all six slots built; parents can change the level).
- **Only once everything is built:** while a mod of your theme is still under construction, the Hall of Fame waits. The page shows how many build orders are still running.
- **Putting heroes in the Hall:** your finished hero stays there forever, with theme, mods, rank and date. Then you start again with a new theme.
- **Nothing is lost:** rank, points, badges and special days stay.
- **Dedication:** parents can write a short text (max. 140 characters) for each hero.
- **Hall of Fame badges:** *First hero*, *Hero trio* (3 heroes), *Collector* (all 10 themes), *Legend* (hero at the highest rank).

---

## 10. Special day

- **What is it?** A personal day with one parent, e.g. an outing, the cinema or going out to eat. You can wish for whatever you like.
- **Unlocking:** you reach a rank and build level 6 in that rank. You get one special day each for ranks 2, 3, 4 and 5.
- **No points price:** your mods are the proof.
- **Wish:** in the pop-up, write your wish and choose who comes along: Mum, Dad or "doesn't matter".
- **Acceptance:** a parent confirms with "Wish accepted". That parent is the companion. Agree on the date outside the app.
- **Limits:** at most 2 special days per child and app year. At least 150 days must lie between two special days.
- **An early unlock is not lost:** it waits as "can be redeemed from [date]".

---

## 11. Family goal

- **Shared goal:** the XP of all kids add up over one week (Monday to Sunday). Starting goal: 450 points.
- **Condition:** every kid who is not on a break needs at least one approved task in the week.
- **Success:** confetti pop-up for the kids and a push to the parents.
- **Progress:** the big bar is shown on the hallway tablet.
- **Choosing the goal:** on Sundays the kids vote for the next week's goal. In a tie, the parents decide.
- **Family rewards:** parents maintain a list (e.g. pizza, game night, cinema, ice cream, barbecue).
- **Big goal:** after 4 weekly goals in a row, a big goal unlocks (e.g. an outing).
- **No penalty:** if you miss the goal, you lose nothing.

---

## 12. Ranking and hero of the week

- **Family ranking:** all kids see the family side by side: avatars, rank, mods, badges and streak.
- **Hero of the week:** the kid with the **biggest gain** compared with their own average.
- **Raw points** are visible but rank lower.
- Parents switch the view in the parent dashboard.
- Parents are not included in the ranking.

---

## 13. Forecast and calibration (for parents)

- **Forecast:** the app uses the average of recent weeks to estimate when the next rank and the next special day will be reached.
- **Traffic light:** shows the deviation from the target (two special days per year).
- **Kids see:** "Next rank expected in about X weeks". This can be switched off.
- **Calibration:** after 4 to 6 weeks, parents should check the values. The app suggests a factor. Applying it takes one click and is recorded in the log.

---

## 14. Settings

**For parents**

- Rules, thresholds, prices, build times, limits and default values. Everything is stored in the database and can be changed.
- Rename ranks.
- Manage tasks, rewards, mods and the family goal.
- Set breaks.
- Manage devices. Parents mark the hallway tablet as **Kiosk (hallway tablet)**.
- View the log: who did what, and when.

**For kids**

- Choose your own avatar and accent colour.
- Turn on notifications (on your own iPhone).

---

## 15. Notifications

- **Parents** are notified straight away of every event involving their kids: by **email** and by **push** on the iPhone.
- **Each parent** chooses which events they see.
- **Quiet hours:** push stays silent. The event still appears in the list.
- **Kids** get push on their own iPhone, e.g. for "Task approved" or "Mod finished".
- **Reminders:** kids get a push reminder 6 hours before a reservation expires. Parents get a message 3 hours before a task that has not been accepted expires.
- The parent dashboard shows all events as a list with a counter.

**Setting up iPhone push:** add the app to your home screen, open it from the icon and tap **Turn on notifications** in the settings. The instructions are in the [installation guide](INSTALLATION.md).
