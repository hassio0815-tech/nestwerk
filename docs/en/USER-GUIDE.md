# Nestwerk: User Guide

🇬🇧 **English** · 🇩🇪 [Deutsch](../BENUTZERHANDBUCH.md)

For **parents** and **kids**. Short, with the most important steps.

The values in this guide are **default values**. Parents can adjust all points, prices and thresholds in the settings.

---

## 1. Basic idea

- **Voluntary:** every task is optional. There is no obligation, no punishment and no point deductions.
- **Taking part pays off:** whoever completes tasks earns points. You can spend points on rewards, mods or special days.
- **You decide:** you choose which tasks you take on and what you spend your points on.
- **Everything stays in the family:** the app runs on your own server in the home network.

---

## 2. Tasks and approval

**For kids**

1. On the kids' dashboard, select a task under **Open tasks**.
2. You accept it: it is reserved for you. Your siblings see it as taken.
3. Done? **Report as done.** If needed, add a photo or a message.
4. Your parents approve it. Then you get the points.

**Several interested kids:** if several kids want the same task, the parents decide who gets it.

**Reservation:** whoever accepts a task holds it reserved. Once the time has run out, it becomes free again. There is no penalty for this.

**For parents**

- Create tasks: title and point value are required. Everything else is optional (time window, repeat, assignment, limit).
- Rate a task: one point is roughly one minute of effort. This is a guideline.
- Approval: **Trust** (points right away), **Parents approve** or **Photo/text proof**.
- Bonus points: after approval, you can give extra points with a short reason (e.g. "very thorough").
- Proofs (photos and texts) are deleted immediately after approval.
- The **task library** contains templates. One click activates them.

### Quick buttons

For tasks that only come up now and then (**General waste is full!**). The task sleeps until someone presses its button.

- **Setting up (parents):** create a task with the repeat option **On demand**, plus an emoji and **Who may press it?** (everyone or parents only). The library includes **General waste**, **Organic waste** and **Recycle glass** (5 points each). The task list marks them **Quick button**.
- **Automatic:** The app creates the three default buttons as tasks once on its own (as soon as parents exist, within an hour at the latest). Deleted ones do not come back. The hallway tablet only shows buttons if it is registered under **Devices** as **Kiosk (hallway tablet)**.
- **Pressing:** exactly **one** open task is created. While it runs, the button shows **already running** (with the name, if someone took it on). A second press does nothing.
- **I'll do it:** the task is reserved for that kid, like when a kid takes on a task normally.
- **Just report it:** the task stays open for all kids, and the kids get a notification ("General waste: who's up for it?"). With "I'll do it" there is no notification, and parents get no push message.
- **Where:** kid dashboard (only buttons for everyone), parent dashboard and hallway tablet. On the tablet you tap the button, then **Just report it** or your picture, **without a PIN**. This only works on the device registered as the hallway tablet, and "parents only" buttons never appear there.

### Recognition (parents)

For something special without a task, for example **Helped Grandma**.

- **Where:** **Recognition** in the quick access or the tasks bar. Choose the child, enter the **points**, then the **note** below (required, the child reads it).
- **Limits:** 1 to 25 points per award, at most 50 points per child per week (Monday to Sunday). Set both under **Settings → Tasks**.
- **Effect:** 1 point = 1 balance and 1 XP. The child gets a message, the points show under **Latest points** and count for the family goal. A reversal frees the room in the week again.

### My settings and moods

- **Gear icon (kids):** at the top of the header. Change your **name**, **nickname** (optional), your **code** and your **mood**. For the code you need the old code and enter the new one twice. Several wrong attempts lock the code for a short while, like at login.
- **Mood:** pick one of 50 moods or **No mood**. It shows as a small line under your name on the start page.
- **Suggest a new mood:** write a short text (up to 40 characters, at most 3 open suggestions). Your parents approve or decline it. Approved moods exist for all kids.
- **Parents:** under **Moods** (quick access, counter under decisions) you see the suggestions, can hide catalogue moods and add your own. You can reset a **child's code** at any time under **Family**.

## 3. Points, balance and XP

- **Points:** every approved task brings points.
- **Balance:** the points you can spend right now. They go down when you redeem something or buy a mod.
- **XP (total points):** they rise with every task and **never fall**. Your rank is based on your XP.

**For kids:** your balance is your wallet. Your XP is your progress.

---

## 4. Rewards and pocket money

- **Reward catalog:** parents set fixed rewards with prices in points. Categories: Sweets, TV time, Gaming and computer time, Pocket money, Other.
- **Redeeming:** you submit a **request**. Both parents are notified right away.
- **Points are deducted only on approval:** the points are taken off only once a parent agrees.
- **Pending requests** already count against your balance. You cannot request more than you have.
- **Pocket money:** there is a **monthly limit** per child. Parents set it.
- Parents unlock devices, gaming or TV time outside the app.

---

## 5. Ranks

There are six ranks, like rarities in a game:

| Rank | From XP (start) | Minimum active weeks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Common | 0 | 0 |
| 2 Uncommon | 2,000 | 20 |
| 3 Rare | 5,000 | 42 |
| 4 Epic | 9,000 | 64 |
| 5 Legendary | 14,000 | 90 |
| 6 God Mode | 20,000 | 120 |

- **Rank up:** you need enough XP **and** enough **active weeks**.
- **Active week:** a week in which at least one of your tasks was approved.
- Active weeks are never reset. Weeks without a task cost nothing, but they don't count either.
- Age doesn't matter. The same rules apply to everyone.
- **God Mode:** after that, every 26 active weeks you get a special day and a new mod set, endlessly.

---

## 6. Streak and break

- **Weekly streak:** every active week in a row counts +1.
- **Streak bonus:** from streak 2 on, you get more points per task. +5% per streak week, up to a maximum of +25%.
- **If a week is missed,** the streak starts again at 0. Points, rank and active weeks stay.
- **Daily streak:** days in a row with an approved task. It earns badges, but no points.
- **Break:** parents set a break for one child or the whole family. Break weeks and days are neutral. The streak does not break. You see "Break – your streak is safe".
- Tasks completed during a break count as normal.

---

## 7. Badges

- **Badges** are awards for streaks, quarters and rank-ups.
- Examples: daily streak of 7, 14 or 30 days. Weekly streak of 1, 2, 4, 8, 12, 24 or 48 weeks. A quarter with active weeks throughout. A badge for every rank-up.
- **Badges give no points.** They appear on your profile. A pop-up announces new badges.

---

## 8. Avatar, mods and building

- **Avatar:** every child has their own block figure. You choose a **theme**: Knight, Space explorer, Ninja, Robot, Pirate, Viking, Footballer, Basketball player, Racing driver, or Jungle or forest explorer.
- **Six slots:** head, body, back, hand, companion and background.
- **Mods** are purely cosmetic. They have no effect on points or rules. You pay for them with your balance.
- **Mod shop:** you buy mods for your rank. A mod can only be bought once you reach the rank whose rarity it has. Prices rise with rarity.
- **Build time:** a bought mod takes time (e.g. 2 days for Common, 7 days for Legendary). Only then does it count toward your **build level**.
- **Build level:** the number of finished mods in your current rank (max. 6). At most one finished mod counts per week.
- **Changing theme:** possible at any time. You keep your rank, but the mods in your slots are lost. A warning shows you the exact numbers beforehand. There is no refund.

---

## 9. Hall of Fame

- **Unlocking:** you reach build level 6 in your current rank (all six slots built; parents can change the level).
- **Only once everything is built:** while a mod of your theme is still under construction, the Hall of Fame waits. The page shows how many build orders are still running.
- **Putting heroes in the Hall:** your finished hero stays there forever, with theme, mods, rank and date. Then you start again with a new theme.
- **Nothing is lost:** rank, points, badges and special days stay.
- **Dedication:** parents can write a short text (max. 140 characters) for each hero.
- **Hall of Fame badges:** *First hero*, *Hero trio* (3 heroes), *Collector* (all 10 themes), *Legend* (hero at the highest rank).

---

## 10. Special day

- **What is it?** A personal day with one parent, e.g. an outing, the cinema or going out to eat. You can wish for whatever you like.
- **Unlocking:** you reach a rank and build level 6 in that rank. You get one special day each for ranks 2, 3, 4 and 5.
- **No points price:** your mods are the proof.
- **Wish:** in the pop-up, write your wish and choose who comes along: Mum, Dad or "doesn't matter".
- **Acceptance:** a parent confirms with "Wish accepted". That parent is the companion. Agree on the date outside the app.
- **Limits:** at most 2 special days per child and app year. At least 150 days must lie between two special days.
- **An early unlock is not lost:** it waits as "can be redeemed from [date]".

---

## 11. Family goal

- **Shared goal:** the XP of all kids add up over one week (Monday to Sunday). Starting goal: 450 points.
- **Condition:** every kid who is not on a break needs at least one approved task in the week.
- **Success:** confetti pop-up for the kids and a push to the parents.
- **Progress:** the big bar is shown on the hallway tablet.
- **Choosing the goal:** on Sundays the kids vote for the next week's goal. In a tie, the parents decide.
- **Family rewards:** parents maintain a list (e.g. pizza, game night, cinema, ice cream, barbecue).
- **Big goal:** after 4 weekly goals in a row, a big goal unlocks (e.g. an outing).
- **No penalty:** if you miss the goal, you lose nothing.

---

## 12. Ranking and hero of the week

- **Family ranking:** all kids see the family side by side: avatars, rank, mods, badges and streak.
- **Hero of the week:** the kid with the **biggest gain** compared with their own average.
- **Raw points** are visible but rank lower.
- Parents switch the view in the parent dashboard.
- Parents are not included in the ranking.

---

## 13. Forecast and calibration (for parents)

- **Forecast:** the app uses the average of recent weeks to estimate when the next rank and the next special day will be reached.
- **Traffic light:** shows the deviation from the target (two special days per year).
- **Kids see:** "Next rank expected in about X weeks". This can be switched off.
- **Calibration:** after 4 to 6 weeks, parents should check the values. The app suggests a factor. Applying it takes one click and is recorded in the log.

---

## 14. Settings

**For parents**

- Rules, thresholds, prices, build times, limits and default values. Everything is stored in the database and can be changed.
- Rename ranks.
- Manage tasks, rewards, mods and the family goal.
- Set breaks.
- Manage devices. Parents mark the hallway tablet as **Kiosk (hallway tablet)**.
- View the log: who did what, and when.

**For kids**

- Choose your own avatar and accent colour.
- Turn on notifications (on your own iPhone).

---

## 15. Notifications

- **Parents** are notified straight away of every event involving their kids: by **email** and by **push** on the iPhone.
- **Each parent** chooses which events they see.
- **Quiet hours:** push stays silent. The event still appears in the list.
- **Kids** get push on their own iPhone, e.g. for "Task approved" or "Mod finished".
- **Reminders:** kids get a push reminder 6 hours before a reservation expires. Parents get a message 3 hours before a task that has not been accepted expires.
- The parent dashboard shows all events as a list with a counter.

**Setting up iPhone push:** add the app to your home screen, open it from the icon and tap **Turn on notifications** in the settings. The instructions are in the [installation guide](INSTALLATION.md).

## 16. Learning

The learning module is **optional** and meant for kids and parents. There are **no grades** and no comparison between the kids. Goals are always achievable.

**Without AI**, practice works with the existing questions (start data: English vocabulary; for maths there is the topic structure, and the questions come from material or from the parents). AI is **off by default**.

### Practice (kids)

- **Where:** the **Learning** tile on the home page. There you see **Your day**.
- **New round:** you choose **what** to practise (subject and topic), **what you plan** (get to know new things, review what is due, practise my stumbling blocks), **how you go about it** (for example say it aloud, or think first and then type), **how much you already know** and **how demanding** it should be (Warm-up, Normal, Challenge).
- **Answers:** you get feedback such as **right**, **almost right** or **not yet**. On "not yet" you can ask for help or look at the solution. Help costs **no points** and is never penalised.
- **Spaced repetition:** what you know well comes back later. What was hard comes back sooner.
- **Too much frustration?** After several mistakes in a row it gets easier, and the system suggests a break.
- **Look back:** at the end you answer two short questions: What was hard? What helped? You also say whether it was too easy, just right or too hard. Parents see this as a hint.

### Daily goal and streak (kids)

- **Daily goal:** default 20 minutes. Even a **minimum day** (default 5 minutes) counts as a practised day.
- **The goal adapts:** after two missed days it gets smaller, without blame. When things go well for a long time, the system suggests raising it a little. That only happens if **you agree**.
- **Weekly streak:** the streak counts in **weeks**. On **rest days** (illness, holidays) nothing is required and the streak stays. A broken streak **never removes points**.
- **Points:** there are learning points for reaching the daily goal (default 5, at most 10 per day), **not per correct answer**. Parents can switch learning points off.

### Uploading material (kids)

- **Where:** **My folder** in the Learning area. Upload a photo or document (image, PDF, text), for example a vocabulary list or a worksheet.
- **What happens:** with AI switched on, Nestwerk reads the text and turns it into practice questions. This takes a moment. The material shows its status ("being read", "questions are ready").
- **Approval:** by default **parents** look at the questions and approve them. For each child parents can allow questions from uploads to be **usable right away**.
- **Who sees the folder?** Only you and your parents, **never** a sibling.
- **Without AI:** the material is stored but not turned into questions.

### Exam preparation (kids)

- **Where:** **Exams** in the Learning area. Say **when** the exam is and **what** it covers (topics or your material).
- **Plan:** Nestwerk plans backwards from the date in small portions. A day never holds more than your daily goal, there are **buffer days**, and the last day before the exam only has light review.
- **Reality check:** if the material does not fit into the time, there is **no** plan that overloads you. Instead you get suggestions (start earlier, less material, raise the goal a little). **You** decide.
- **Mock exam:** a practice run with questions from the material. It shows where things still wobble and is **not a grade**.
- There are **points** for keeping to exam plans (amounts in the settings).

### Tutor (kids and parents)

- **What it does:** the tutor **explains** new things and **helps you solve** your tasks with a ladder of help steps. Each step only comes when **you** ask for more help: ask back, a nudge, a similar example with other numbers or words, a pointer to your material, a solution outline without the result.
- **What it does not do:** it **does not solve your homework** and does not give solutions. It only talks about learning material from your folder. It checks your own attempts.
- **Off by default.** Parents switch it on **for the whole family** (parents: **Learning → Tutor**). It needs a switched-on AI for the task "Tutor".
- **Log:** parents can read the conversations. Conversations without activity are deleted after some time (default 90 days). There is a daily limit.
- **Honest limits (parents, please read):**
  - There is **no guarantee**. A child can copy a task or rephrase it, and an AI can make mistakes.
  - The safeguards are: no model solution in the conversation, a check of every answer for leaked solutions, a daily limit and the log. The check is a **heuristic**, not a watertight control.
  - Look at the log from time to time.

### Practice week and titles

- In the family ranking and "Hero of the Week" only **participation** counts (on how many days someone practised), **never the amount**.
- A family goal can read: "everyone practises on four days".
- **Weekly titles** come without points: Practice Master, Climber, Stayer, Explorer. Each value is relative to **your own** goal. On a tie the kids share the title. The titles are only a display. Parents can switch them off under **Learning → Weekly picture**.

### For parents

- **Where:** parents area, **Learning** tile.
- **Learning folder and approval:** look at the kids' material, **approve** or reject questions, check storage. Files in the archive stay until you delete them. The space per child is limited (default 500 MB).
- **Log:** read tutor conversations (**Learning → Tutor**). The general log (section 14) shows who set up, switched on or assigned an AI provider and who changed children's details or the new school year. The log contains no keys and no school names.
- **Weekly picture** (**Learning → Weekly picture**): per child days practised, help steps used over time, weak spots and short **suggestions** for what you can do. No grades, no sibling comparison.
- **Exams:** you see the kids' plans (read only). You can change a date or delete an exam.
- **Rest days:** enter pauses for one child or all (illness, holidays, other). Then no goal is required and the streak stays.
- **Children and levels** (**Learning → Children and levels**): change grade, school type, level per subject (for example basic or extended level) and the approval of upload questions. Each child is saved on its own. This is optional and stays on your server.
  - **New school year:** the button raises **all entered grades by 1**, up to 13 at most. A confirmation with numbers comes first. It only changes the grade, **never** levels, learning progress or material. Children without a grade stay without one.
- **Setting up an AI provider** (**Learning → AI connection**):
  1. **New provider:** enter name, type, address, model name and, if needed, a key. The provider starts **switched off**. Nestwerk never shows the key again.
  2. **Test connection:** the test only sends "Reply with OK", no data about the kids.
  3. **Switch on:** for a **cloud provider** you must confirm the notes first.
  4. **Assignment:** decides which provider does which task (reading, creating questions, checking answers, tutor). This applies to the whole family. Without an assignment, AI stays off for that task. There is no automatic fallback.
  - **Risks:** with **cloud providers**, texts and photos of the kids (for example homework and answers) go to the provider. This is your deliberate decision. Cloud providers usually bill per use. **Any AI can make mistakes**, your own server too. Check outputs before you rely on them.
  - **Own server:** data stays on your network if the server is there. How to connect it is in the installation guide (section 6.3).
  - **Not tested:** the connection is only tested here with a fake transport. Whether your provider and model give good results you have to try yourself.
- **Settings:** minimum day, daily goal, learning points and daily limit, box intervals, upload size per file, storage limit, tutor limits, weekly picture thresholds, AI limits and more are under **Settings**. You switch on holidays for everyone there.
